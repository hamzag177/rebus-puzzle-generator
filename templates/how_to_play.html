How to Play

1. Read the clues

Each puzzle shows a row of pictures and/or text pieces. Say each piece out loud, in order, and the sounds blend together into a word or phrase. For example, a picture of an eye next to the word CREAM sounds like "ice cream."

2. Type your guess

Type the full word or phrase you think the clues spell out into the text box and hit Guess. You get 3 tries per puzzle.

3. Use a hint if you're stuck

Click Hint to see blanks showing the length of each word in the answer -- click it again and the first letter gets revealed too. You get up to 2 hints per puzzle, and the button keeps track of how many you've used.

4. Skip if you want

Not feeling a puzzle? Click Skip to move on to the next one without being marked wrong forever -- it just moves on without counting as a solve.

5. Keep playing

Solve the daily puzzle and you can keep going with the rest of the puzzle list, or head to Browse Puzzles any time to jump straight into any individual puzzle.
